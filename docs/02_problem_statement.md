# 2. Problem Statement

Agricultural motor pumps and their associated copper cables are frequently installed in remote and unattended farm locations, making them highly susceptible to theft and unauthorized tampering. The absence of continuous monitoring and real-time alert mechanisms leads to delayed detection, resulting in significant financial losses, irrigation disruption, and reduced agricultural productivity.

Existing solutions such as CCTV systems and manual surveillance are either cost-prohibitive or impractical in rural environments due to power and maintenance constraints. Therefore, there is a need to develop a smart, low-cost, and reliable surveillance system capable of detecting motor and cable theft in real time while providing immediate alerts and location tracking for effective response and recovery.

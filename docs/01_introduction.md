# 1. Introduction

Agriculture plays a vital role in sustaining rural economies, where irrigation systems are essential for maintaining crop productivity. Water pump motors are widely used in farms to ensure a continuous supply of water. However, these motors and their associated copper cables are often installed in remote and unattended locations, making them highly vulnerable to theft and tampering.

Copper cable theft has emerged as a significant issue due to the high resale value of copper in scrap markets. The theft of motor components and wiring not only results in direct financial losses for farmers but also disrupts irrigation schedules, leading to reduced agricultural output and long-term economic impact. Additionally, the absence of real-time monitoring systems makes it difficult for farmers to detect and respond to such incidents promptly.

Existing security solutions such as CCTV surveillance and manual guarding are often impractical in rural environments due to high cost, power requirements, and maintenance challenges. Therefore, there is a need for a low-cost, reliable, and energy-efficient system that can continuously monitor agricultural infrastructure and provide instant alerts in case of theft or tampering.

The FarmSecureX system is proposed as a smart surveillance framework that leverages embedded systems and communication technologies to address these challenges. By integrating a coded continuity-based sensing mechanism with GSM and GPS modules, the system enables real-time detection, alerting, and tracking of theft incidents. The solution is designed to be scalable, affordable, and suitable for deployment in rural and off-grid environments.

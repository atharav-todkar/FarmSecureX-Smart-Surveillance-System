# 3. Literature Review

## 3.1 Overview

The problem of agricultural motor and copper cable theft has been widely observed in rural and semi-urban areas. Several research works and practical solutions have been proposed to address this issue using embedded systems, communication technologies, and IoT-based monitoring.

---

## 3.2 Existing Solutions

### 1. GSM-Based Alert Systems

Many systems use GSM modules to send alerts when abnormal conditions are detected.

**Limitation:**

* Detects only after theft occurs
* Does not ensure continuous cable monitoring

---

### 2. IoT-Based Surveillance Systems

IoT-based solutions use sensors and internet connectivity to monitor farm conditions.

**Limitation:**

* Requires internet connectivity
* High cost and complexity for rural deployment

---

### 3. CCTV Surveillance Systems

CCTV cameras are used for visual monitoring.

**Limitation:**

* High installation cost
* Requires continuous power supply
* Not suitable for remote farms

---

### 4. MAX485-Based Communication Systems

Some systems use MAX485 for long-distance communication and detection.

**Limitation:**

* Limited intelligence
* No classification of theft type

---

## 3.3 Research Gap

From the analysis of existing systems, the following gaps are identified:

* Lack of low-cost solutions suitable for rural environments
* Absence of continuous cable integrity monitoring
* No accurate classification between cable theft and motor theft
* Limited real-time response capability

---

## 3.4 Proposed Contribution

The FarmSecureX system addresses these gaps by introducing:

* A coded continuity-based sensing mechanism
* Real-time detection and alert system
* Gyroscope-based theft classification
* GPS-based tracking for recovery
* Low-cost and scalable design

---

## 3.5 Conclusion

The literature review highlights the limitations of existing solutions and justifies the need for an improved system. The proposed FarmSecureX framework provides a reliable, cost-effective, and intelligent solution for agricultural theft detection.
